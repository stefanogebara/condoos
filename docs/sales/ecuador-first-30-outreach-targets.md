# Ecuador First 30 Outreach Targets

Prepared: 2026-06-10  
Use: first private CONDOS sales campaign in Ecuador.  
Rule: quality replies and demos matter more than send volume.

## Campaign Thesis

The first Ecuador campaign should not sell "software." It should sell operational calm:

- Fewer calls and WhatsApps between residents, guards, admins, and boards.
- Cleaner visitor, party, delivery, and package flow.
- More transparent alicuotas, payment proofs, expenses, and receipts.
- Faster maintenance follow-up with visible evidence.
- A private activation model for serious buildings, not open public signup.

The best first buyer is still a professional administrator or building board with visible operational pain. Direct buildings are attractive, but the decision maker is often hidden, so the first task is to identify the administrator or board president through public sales/admin routes.

## Campaign Operating Rules

- Do not send generic copy to all 30.
- Touch 1 should include one concrete reason we chose them.
- If the decision maker is hidden, the CTA is discovery: "¿quién ve tecnología/operaciones para la administración?"
- For direct buildings, avoid messaging individual renters or guests. Use admin, developer, broker, lobby, or owner-committee paths.
- Keep WhatsApp short. Use email for the full story.
- Track every reply and objection before increasing volume.
