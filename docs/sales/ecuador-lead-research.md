# Ecuador Lead Research - Condo Management Agencies

Research date: 2026-06-09  
Focus: private B2B sales for CONDOS, starting with Ecuador management agencies and selected condo/urbanization operators.

## Executive Summary

Ecuador has a strong first wedge for CONDOS: professional administrators of buildings, condominiums, conjuntos, and urbanizaciones. The highest-quality prospects are already selling the same pain points CONDOS solves: transparency, alicuota collection, maintenance control, resident communication, security/guard operations, visitor access, document control, assemblies, and reporting.

The best first outreach segment is not individual residents. It is agencies already managing multiple buildings or high-value urbanizations, especially:

- Quito, Cumbaya, Tumbaco, Puembo, Valle de los Chillos: vertical residential buildings and premium conjuntos.
- Guayaquil, Daule, Samborondon, La Aurora: larger urbanizations with guards, resident entry, deliveries, parties, alicuotas, and high communication load.
- Channel partners: condominium training/legal/collection organizations that influence administrators and boards.

## Lead Scoring Rubric

Score is 0-100:

- ICP fit, 30 pts: directly manages condominiums, edificios, conjuntos, urbanizaciones, or residential portfolios.
- Pain alignment, 25 pts: public signals around maintenance, dues, transparency, resident communication, security, visitors, or reports.
- Reachability, 15 pts: public website, email, phone, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or active social.
- Buying leverage, 15 pts: portfolio size, multi-city coverage, followers, years in market, or visible credibility.
- Technology angle, 10 pts: uses software already, talks about digital tools, or has clear operational gaps CONDOS can improve.
- Strategic value, 5 pts: potential as reference customer, partner, or access to many buildings.

Confidence:

- High: company has official website/LinkedIn and direct service evidence.
- Medium: strong social/directory evidence but less official detail.
- Low: useful lead but requires manual verification before outreach.

## Recommended Outreach Segments

### Segment A - Management agencies with portfolios

Use for: ADMTOSCANO, Kondominios, Administra 360, HABITAR, MyConcierge, ADMIS, Solventtar, Condominios Ecuador.

Message angle:

"We are building CONDOS as a private operating system for building administrators: guard/visitor flow, resident requests, dues/payment proof, maintenance work orders, documents, proposals, and board transparency. I saw you already manage [buildings/urbanizations/condos] and talk about [specific pain]. Could I show you a 15-minute private demo focused on reducing WhatsApps/calls and giving the board better visibility?"

### Segment B - Enterprise residential / property operators

Use for: REMS, DCL.

Message angle:

"CONDOS is not another resident chat app. It is an operations layer for residential portfolios: cross-building alerts, audit trails, resident/guard/admin workflows, file evidence, board packets, and service transparency."

### Segment C - Finance/accounting/legal specialists

Use for: Contabilidad de Servicios, Cobrapoco, Polibienes.

Message angle:

"CONDOS can make the money side easier to trust: alicuota status, payment proof review, receipts, expense explanations, budget vs actual, and exportable evidence for boards/legal follow-up."

### Segment D - Channel partners

Use for: Fundacion Bien-Estar, CCQ.

Message angle:

"We want to run a practical session for administrators and board members: how to reduce guard/resident confusion, improve transparency, and document decisions. CONDOS can sponsor/show a real demo, not a generic software talk."

## Buying Signals To Watch

Strong positive signals:

- They mention alicuotas, morosidad, financial transparency, budgets, or reportes.
- They manage urbanizaciones or buildings with guards/security.
- They mention maintenance logs, vendor coordination, emergencies, or 24/7 attention.
- They have multiple buildings, many followers, or public client references.
- They already use digital tools but still have visible workflow gaps.
- They post educational content about bad administration, disorder, or resident conflict.

Disqualifiers / weak-fit signals:

- Only short-term rental management.
- Only real estate sales/brokerage with no building operations.
- No clear active channels since before 2024.
- Suspended/unclear legal status.
- Pure software competitors unless approached as benchmarks, not prospects.

## First Campaign Plan

### Week 1: Manual qualification and warm messages

- Verify contacts for the top 10.
- Find decision makers on LinkedIn/Instagram/website.
- Send 10 personalized messages, not a blast.
- CTA: "15-minute private demo focused on reducing guard/resident WhatsApps and board confusion."

### Week 2: Second layer

- Contact ranks 11-20.
- Add channel outreach to Fundacion Bien-Estar and CCQ.
- Offer one free pilot analysis: "We map one building's current visitor, maintenance, and payment-proof flow and show where calls/WhatsApps disappear."

### Week 3: Pilot push

- Ask the most engaged 3-5 prospects for a private pilot with one building.
- Offer limited "founding Ecuador partner" positioning, not a discount-only pitch.
- Record objections and map them to product gaps.

## What To Prepare Before Outreach

- 15-minute Spanish demo script for agency managers.
- One-page PDF: "CONDOS para administradoras de condominios en Ecuador."
- Two demo paths:
  - Urbanization with guard, visitors, deliveries, parties, packages.
  - Building with dues, payment proof, tickets/work orders, documents, board reports.
- Case-style deck using fake but realistic Ecuador building names.
- Landing page CTA: "Solicitar demo privada" rather than public self-serve signup.

## Research Notes And Uncertainty

- Some leads are social-only and need manual verification before sending serious outreach.
- Some tech-forward administrators may already use software. That is not automatically bad; it means the buyer understands the category. The pitch must be about replacing fragmented workflows and making guard/admin/resident flows tighter.
- Habitanto/Rezydenz/MiBarrioFacil/Urbalinks are competitor intelligence unless a partnership angle appears.
- The most promising short-term client type is the administrator frustrated by WhatsApp chaos and board transparency demands, not a software competitor.
