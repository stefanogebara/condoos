# CONDOS Private B2B Outreach Playbook

CONDOS should be sold privately to condo management agencies and selected
buildings. The first sales message is not "another resident portal." It is:

> Reduce front-desk confusion, resident WhatsApps, unresolved maintenance, and
> board conflict.

Use this playbook after the pre-outreach readiness gate is green.

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Best First Customers

- Condo management agencies managing multiple buildings.
- Self-managed condo boards with a staffed front desk or guard.
- Buildings with recurring maintenance, visitor, package, or money transparency
  pain.
- Buildings already using WhatsApp, spreadsheets, shared drives, or paper logs
  to run operations.

### Strong Buying Signals

- Public website mentions condominium administration, building operations, or
  resident services.
- Multiple managed buildings or visible portfolio pages.
- Online reviews mention communication delays, maintenance delays, front-desk
  confusion, visitor access, payment confusion, or board transparency.
- Agency posts about hiring operations, concierge, maintenance, or finance
  staff.
- Building has a guard/front desk and frequent visitor/package activity.
- Board or agency uses manual channels for resident requests and notices.
- Contact page has operations, finance, or building manager roles.

### Disqualifiers

- No front desk, no guard, and very low visitor/package volume.
- One tiny building with no budget and no board urgency.
- Buyer wants full ERP/accounting/payroll replacement on day one.
- Buyer refuses controlled onboarding or basic data cleanup.
- No one owns decisions for operations, finance, or resident communication.

## Buyer Personas

### Agency Owner Or Director

Pain: too many calls, unhappy boards, staff overload, weak reporting.

Promise: see every building's risk, follow-ups, vendor issues, dues visibility,
and board reports from one place.

### Building Administrator

Pain: residents ask the same questions, tickets disappear, vendors are hard to
track, money explanations take too much time.

Promise: command center, work orders, receipts, resident transparency, and clear
next actions.

### Board President

Pain: residents distrust expenses, votes lack information, meetings are messy.

Promise: transparent money, proposal readiness, documents, votes, and monthly
board packets.

### Guard Or Concierge Supervisor

Pain: visitor lists are scattered, residents do not answer, packages pile up.

Promise: one fast screen for expected visitors, parties, packages, deliveries,
resident contacts, and arrival history.

## Lead Sources

Start with targeted, signal-based lists instead of scraping thousands of weak
contacts.

- Google Maps: "administracion de condominios", "administradora de condominios",
  "administradora de edificios", "administracao de condominios",
  "sindicatura profissional", "condominio administradora".
- LinkedIn: agency owners, operations managers, property managers, building
  administrators, and condominium administrators in Brazil and Ecuador.
- Local agency websites: capture portfolio size, services, city, contact form,
  phone, WhatsApp, and leadership names.
- Building vendor network: guards, elevator companies, cleaning companies,
  security companies, and maintenance vendors often know which buildings are
  disorganized.
- Referrals: ask board members, residents, vendors, and administrators for one
  building where WhatsApp chaos is obvious.
- Local associations/events: condominium administration groups, real estate
  management communities, board education events, and property tech meetups.

## Lead Scoring

Score each account from 0 to 10 before outreach.

| Signal | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Manages 5+ buildings or has a visible portfolio | 2 |
| Has staffed operations/front desk/security services | 2 |
| Public reviews mention communication/maintenance/payment pain | 2 |
| Has clear decision-maker contact | 1 |
| Active WhatsApp/contact channel | 1 |
| Located in Brazil or Ecuador target city | 1 |
| Has board/reporting/finance services on website | 1 |

Tier accounts:

- Tier 1: 8-10 points. Personal research, custom email, LinkedIn, call, and
  short video.
- Tier 2: 5-7 points. Personalized email plus follow-up.
- Tier 3: 3-4 points. Nurture or referral ask.
- Below 3: skip for now.

## Core Offer

Use a low-friction CTA:

"Can I show you a 15-minute walkthrough of how CONDOS reduces visitor confusion,
resident messages, unresolved maintenance, and board reporting work?"

Do not lead with every feature. Lead with one operational story:

1. Resident pre-approves visitor or party.
2. Guard sees exactly who can enter.
3. Resident reports issue.
4. Admin assigns vendor and tracks work.
5. Finance attaches receipt and explanation.
6. Board gets a monthly report.

## 15-Minute Demo Script

Minute 0-2: Frame the pain.

- "CONDOS reduces calls, WhatsApps, front-desk confusion, maintenance delays,
  and board conflict."

Minute 2-5: Visitor and guard flow.

- Resident creates visitor or party list.
- Guard sees expected arrivals and resident phone fallback.
- Guard notifies resident for unapproved arrivals.

Minute 5-8: Maintenance flow.

- Resident reports issue.
- Neighbors verify.
- Admin reviews, assigns vendor, tracks quote/work order.
- Agency portfolio flags stale vendor follow-up.

Minute 8-11: Money trust.

- Show invoice/payment proof.
- Show expense with receipt and explanation.
- Show budget/reporting view.

Minute 11-13: Agency command center.

- Show portfolio health, attention queue, escalations, vendor intelligence, and
  monthly report.

Minute 13-15: Pilot ask.

- "If this matches one or two buildings you manage, the next step is a small
  private pilot with controlled setup codes, one admin, one guard, and a group of
  residents."

## Pilot Offer

Recommended first offer:

- 1 or 2 buildings.
- 30 days.
- Controlled private activation.
- One agency/admin owner.
- One guard or concierge user.
- A small resident group first, then the full building.
- Success metrics agreed before start.

Pilot success metrics:

- Visitor approvals handled in app.
- Packages/deliveries logged.
- Tickets created and moved through work-order status.
- Expenses/receipts visible to board/residents where appropriate.
- Monthly board/agency report generated.
- Fewer manual calls or WhatsApps for the tested workflow.

## Objection Handling

"We already use WhatsApp."

CONDOS does not try to delete WhatsApp on day one. It makes the operational
record reliable, then WhatsApp can become a notification layer instead of the
source of truth.

"We already have accounting software."

Good. CONDOS should not replace full accounting first. It starts with resident
visibility, receipts, payment proof, budget clarity, and exports.

"Our guards are not technical."

That is exactly why guard mode is one simple screen: search, today's arrivals,
packages, parties, and notify/call resident.

"Residents will not adopt another app."

Start with high-value actions: visitor approvals, packages, reservations,
payments/proofs, and ticket updates. Adoption should follow daily utility, not
feature lists.

"Can this run all buildings immediately?"

Start with one or two buildings, prove the workflow, then expand. Agency
portfolio mode is designed for multi-building operations, but rollout should be
controlled.
